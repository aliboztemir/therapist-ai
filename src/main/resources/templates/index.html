⚠️ Terms of Use & Informed Consent

Please read carefully before using TherapistAI.

1. Nature of the Service

TherapistAI is an experimental, AI-powered conversational assistant developed as a university graduation thesis project. It is not a medical, psychological, psychiatric, or therapeutic service and must not be used as a substitute for professional mental health care.

2. No Professional Relationship

Interacting with TherapistAI does not create a therapist–client, doctor–patient, or any other professional relationship. The information provided is for informational and research purposes only.

3. Crisis & Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or any other emergency, do not use this application. Instead, please contact:

  • Emergency services: 112 (EU) / 911 (US)
  • Crisis hotlines in your country
  • A licensed mental health professional

4. Data & Privacy

Conversation data may be stored to provide context-aware responses. Do not share sensitive personal information such as ID numbers or financial data.

5. Limitations & Accuracy

AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate for your situation. Always verify important information with a qualified professional.

6. Voluntary Use

Use of this service is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing the page or starting a new conversation.
